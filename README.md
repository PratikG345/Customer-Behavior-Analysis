# Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

> **End-to-end data analytics project** using Python, PostgreSQL, and
> Power BI to explore customer purchasing patterns, evaluate customer
> segments, and translate transaction data into actionable business
> recommendations.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes **3,900 customer transactions** across multiple
product categories. The objective is to understand customer spending
patterns, product preferences, discount usage, shipping choices, and
subscription behavior---and present the findings in a form that can
support business decisions.

The project follows a practical analytics workflow:

1.  Data loading, exploration, and cleaning with Python.
2.  Feature engineering and data preparation with pandas.
3.  Loading the cleaned data into PostgreSQL.
4.  Answering business questions using SQL.
5.  Building an interactive Power BI dashboard.
6.  Translating analytical findings into business recommendations.

## Business Objectives

-   Compare revenue generated by different customer groups.
-   Understand the relationship between discounts and purchase behavior.
-   Identify highly rated and frequently purchased products.
-   Compare purchase patterns across shipping types.
-   Evaluate subscription and repeat-purchase behavior.
-   Segment customers based on their purchase history.
-   Examine revenue contribution across customer age groups.

## Project Workflow

### 1. Data Exploration & Cleaning --- Python

The dataset was loaded and explored using pandas.

Key activities:

-   Inspected dataset structure using `df.info()`.
-   Reviewed descriptive statistics using `df.describe()`.
-   Checked for missing values.
-   Imputed the 37 missing `review_rating` values using the **median
    rating within each product category**.
-   Standardized column names to `snake_case`.
-   Created an `age_group` feature by grouping customer ages.
-   Created `purchase_frequency_days` from purchase data.
-   Checked whether `discount_applied` and `promo_code_used` contained
    redundant information; dropped `promo_code_used`.
-   Connected Python to PostgreSQL and loaded the cleaned DataFrame for
    SQL analysis.

### 2. Business Analysis --- PostgreSQL

SQL was used to investigate ten business questions:

1.  **Revenue by Gender** --- Compared total revenue generated by male
    and female customers.
2.  **High-Spending Discount Users** --- Identified customers who used
    discounts but spent above the average purchase amount.
3.  **Top 5 Products by Rating** --- Ranked products by average review
    rating.
4.  **Shipping Type Comparison** --- Compared average purchase amounts
    for Standard and Express shipping.
5.  **Subscribers vs. Non-Subscribers** --- Compared customer counts,
    average spend, and total revenue by subscription status.
6.  **Discount-Dependent Products** --- Identified products with the
    highest percentage of discounted purchases.
7.  **Customer Segmentation** --- Classified customers as New,
    Returning, or Loyal based on purchase history.
8.  **Top 3 Products per Category** --- Ranked the most purchased
    products within each category.
9.  **Repeat Buyers & Subscriptions** --- Examined subscription status
    among customers with more than five purchases.
10. **Revenue by Age Group** --- Calculated the revenue contribution of
    each age group.

### 3. Interactive Dashboard --- Power BI

An interactive **Customer Behavior Dashboard** was developed to present
the analysis visually.

The dashboard includes:

-   KPI cards for customer count, average purchase amount, and average
    review rating.
-   Customer distribution by subscription status.
-   Revenue by product category.
-   Sales by product category.
-   Revenue by age group.
-   Sales by age group.
-   Slicers for subscription status, gender, category, and shipping
    type.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the project proposes the following actions:

1.  **Boost subscriptions:** Promote exclusive benefits to encourage
    more customers to subscribe.
2.  **Strengthen customer loyalty:** Introduce loyalty programs and
    rewards to encourage repeat purchases and deepen customer
    engagement.
3.  **Review discount policy:** Evaluate discount-heavy products and
    balance promotional activity with margin control.
4.  **Improve product positioning:** Feature highly rated and
    best-selling products in marketing campaigns.
5.  **Use targeted marketing:** Tailor campaigns using customer age
    groups, product preferences, and observed shipping and purchasing
    patterns.

## How to Explore the Project

1.  Clone or download this repository.
2.  Open the Python notebook in Jupyter Notebook, JupyterLab, or Google
    Colab.
3.  Install the required Python packages used in the notebook, such as
    pandas and the PostgreSQL database connector.
4.  Configure your PostgreSQL connection and load the cleaned dataset.
5.  Run the SQL queries in the `sql/` folder against the prepared table.
6.  Open the Power BI `.pbix` file in Power BI Desktop to explore the
    dashboard.

> **Note:** Database credentials and machine-specific connection
